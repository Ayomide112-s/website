About Our Forum

Our Mission & Vision

The Progressive Yoruba Elite Forum is dedicated to preserving and promoting the rich cultural heritage, language, and traditions of the Yoruba people while fostering excellence and progress within our community.

We bring together accomplished Yoruba professionals, entrepreneurs, and community leaders to drive positive change, support youth development, and celebrate our unique identity.
